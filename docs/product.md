# Product walkthrough

## The problem

IELTS learners often spread exercises, answer keys, notes and recordings across
many tools. Momentum connects the practice session to its saved result and the
next review, without claiming that a short exercise proves mastery.

## The journey

**Overview → choose a skill → practise → save → submit → review → return**

### Reading

The library offers complete three-Part, 40-question papers and shorter practice
within a complete paper. The desktop desk separates passage and questions;
smaller screens switch between them. Highlights are not automatically treated as
assistance. Practice and timed examination conditions remain distinct.

### Listening

Guided practice permits support. Independent practice records its own conditions.
Audio playback, answers and draft recovery are part of the same study desk.
After submission, the learner can compare responses with explanations and
timestamped evidence where available.

### Writing

Task 1/2 prompts include planning guidance. Learners can save a draft, submit a
response and add reflection separately. A later reflection must not overwrite the
submitted original.

### Speaking

Learners prepare notes, record only after choosing to do so, listen back and save
a recording to their private account when online storage is configured. Browser
microphone support and permission are required. Denying permission must not erase
the learner's notes or pretend that a recording exists.

## History, review and progress

- History distinguishes a draft from a submitted response.
- Review directs learners to evidence, vocabulary and self-reflection.
- Progress describes the available practice evidence and its limits.
- No independent success is inferred from an assisted retry.
- Writing/Speaking self-review is not an examiner evaluation.

## Current limitations

This is a live beta and a personal software project, not an official IELTS
service or an externally calibrated assessment.

- Short Listening units use synthesized audio; they do not reproduce the full
  test or a genuinely diverse speaker/accent collection.
- There is no automatic band prediction or examiner-certified marking.
- Content availability depends on source approval.
- Cloud functionality requires a configured service and an internet connection.
- Automated browser checks do not replace human usability/accessibility testing
  or every physical device/microphone combination.

## Direction

Continue improving clarity, error recovery and the quality of explanations before
adding more dashboard metrics. Prefer measured learning activity over simulated
scores, and preserve the learner's work through interface changes.
